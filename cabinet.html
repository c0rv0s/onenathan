Inside the glass.

This first study combines AI-generated specimen artwork with a small simulation running in your browser.

Your words choose between three authored worlds: a garden, a pool, and a light left on at home. Each memory gives the drifting particles their own movement and color.

There is no live AI generation yet. The artwork is fixed; the atmosphere responds. Memories are saved only on this device and aren't sent to a server.

I'm interested in what happens when these worlds can grow their own histories. This is the beginning.
